## Executive summary

- **The category is real and growing, but the data on "24/7 automat shops" specifically is thin.**
  German vending overall turned over roughly €3.4 bn in goods (€4.62 bn incl. services) with +18.7 %
  year on year, about 621,000 machines and ~12 million sales a day, 55 % of transactions cashless [S1].
  Nobody publishes a reliable count of walk-in automat shops; treat any "X thousand Automatenkioske"
  claim you see online as unverified.
- **Bavaria changed the rules in AlpenBox24's favour.** Since 1 August 2025 the Bavarian
  Ladenschlussgesetz (BayLadSchlG) lets staffless small supermarkets up to 150 m² open during general
  closing times including Sundays and holidays; municipalities may restrict Sunday/holiday hours by
  ordinance but not below eight consecutive hours [S4, S5, S6]. Regensburg has already used that power
  (court allowed it, July 2026), and the question of Sunday opening of smart stores has reached the
  Federal Constitutional Court [S9]. **Hours must therefore be data, not copy** (see R5 in CLAUDE.md).
- **Location is strong.** Bayrischzeller Straße is the B 472, a four-lane primary road through Miesbach
  [S12]. Miesbach (11,573 residents [S13]) is the Kreisstadt of a district that set a tourism record in
  2025: 805,163 guest arrivals and 2.62 m overnight stays [S14]. The RB 55 rail line Munich–Bayrischzell
  stops in Miesbach and continues to Hausham, Schliersee and Bayrischzell [S15]. The district hospital
  (Agatharied, Hausham) is about 2 km away [S16] — shift workers are a natural night audience.
- **Direct competition in Miesbach town for a general 24/7 snack/drinks shop: none found.** The region's
  own list of self-service shops and machines (Ökomodellregion, as of 6 Aug 2025) shows 44 entries in the
  district, but in Miesbach town they are a dairy tap (7–20 h), a cheese machine, a farm shop (6–21 h) and
  a member-only 24/7 co-op [S17]. The Südkäse cheese machine is on the same street, opposite the Esso
  station, open every day of the year [S18] — a neighbour, and a potential partner, not a rival.
  *Limit of this finding:* web research cannot see machines that have no online footprint. Confirm on the ground.
- **The competitor websites we audited are weak** (single page, no hours block, placeholder text, broken
  social links, no explanation of age checks or payment). The bar to be the best site in this niche is low;
  the bar to be *legally clean* is higher than most competitors realise (tobacco advertising law).
- **The single biggest website risk is advertising vapes online.** § 19 TabakerzG bans advertising for
  e-cigarettes and refill containers in information-society services; 2026 appellate rulings treated
  everyday sales phrasing and colourful banners as prohibited advertising [S20, S21, S22].

**Read-across for AlpenBox24:** the national numbers are dominated by office coffee machines. The
relevant sub-trend — walk-in, staffless, multi-machine shops — is described by the industry body only
qualitatively ("being tested, especially by food retailers") [S1]. Press coverage since 2024 shows the
format spreading in Bavaria and Baden-Württemberg (e.g. a 29-machine "24 Stunden Automaten Supermarkt"
in Munich, ~€500k investment, opened June 2024 [S10]; Kreis Esslingen trend piece, March 2024 [S11]).

**Positioning gap in the format:** the Munich operators explicitly positioned against "typical e-kiosks
that focus on alcohol and imported sweets" [S10]. Municipalities (e.g. Bremen, Aug 2025) scrutinise
these shops mainly for youth protection [S19]. A shop that leads with *regional food and everyday needs*
and is visibly strict on age checks is both more defensible and more marketable.

## 2. Demand drivers at this location

1. **Through-traffic on the B 472** — Miesbach sits between the A8 (exits Weyarn/Irschenberg) and the
   Schliersee/Tegernsee lakes [S16]. Day-trippers and ski/hiking traffic pass through early and late.
2. **Tourism** — district 2025: 805,163 arrivals (+1.7 %), 2,620,889 overnight stays (record), average
   stay 3.26 days; Schliersee had the most arrivals of any municipality (171,995); international share
   7.4 % and rising [S14]. Holiday-flat guests arriving after shop hours need water, breakfast basics, snacks.
3. **Residents outside shop hours** — Bavarian shops close at 20:00 Mon–Sat and on Sundays (general
   closing times, Art. 2 Abs. 1 BayLadSchlG) [S6]. AlpenBox24 covers evenings, nights, Sundays, holidays.
4. **Shift workers** — Krankenhaus Agatharied ~2 km [S16]; plus fuel-station staff, police, care services.
5. **Train users** — RB 55 stops in Miesbach [S15]; late arrivals from Munich.
6. **Regional-food culture** — the district runs an Ökomodellregion programme and a dense farm-gate
   machine culture (milk, eggs, cheese, meat, honey, bread) [S17]. Locals already trust buying food from
   machines. AlpenBox24 can be the curated, central, always-open version.

**Search demand — honest status.** We had no access to Google Keyword Planner, Search Console or a
paid keyword tool in this research session, so **no search-volume numbers are claimed**. For a town of
~11,600, absolute volumes for "[term] Miesbach" will be small; most discovery for "open now / near me"
intent happens in Google Maps through the Business Profile. The website's job is to make that profile
rank and convert, and to capture the long tail (Sunday shopping, regional products, tourists). Validation
plan in `02-seo-strategy.md` §7.

## 4. Regulation that shapes the website and the business

### 4.1 Opening hours — Bavaria (BayLadSchlG, in force 1 Aug 2025)
- Art. 2 Abs. 2 S. 1: staffless small supermarkets (sales area ≤ 150 m², no personal customer contact,
  selection/handover/payment by machines or self-service) may open during general closing times [S5, S6].
- Art. 2 Abs. 2 S. 2: municipalities may set duration and timing of Sunday/holiday opening by ordinance,
  minimum eight consecutive hours [S5, S6]. → Ask Stadt Miesbach (client input #18).
- Art. 9 Abs. 5: no employees may work in such stores during general closing times — including refilling,
  cleaning, maintenance [S5]. → **Operational note for the client:** restock Mon–Sat 6–20 h.
- Art. 2 Abs. 3 S. 2 Nr. 2: individual vending machines outside a Verkaufsstelle with a limited range are
  treated separately [S6]. If AlpenBox24 is an outdoor machine wall rather than a room, a different
  analysis applies (client input #6).
- Context: OVG Münster held a staffless automat kiosk in Bonn is not covered by NRW's shop law [S7]; Bremen's
  Senate (Aug 2025) states vending machines have been exempt from federal closing rules since 1962 [S19].

### 4.2 Youth protection (JuSchG)
- § 10 Abs. 2 + 4: tobacco, nicotine products **and nicotine-free e-cigarettes/e-shishas** may not be offered
  in public vending machines unless the machine is inaccessible to minors or technical devices/constant
  supervision prevent minors from taking them [S27].
- § 9 Abs. 3: same structure for alcoholic drinks (inaccessible place, or commercial room with technical
  device/supervision) [S28].
- In practice operators verify via ID card reader or girocard age feature; some use ID + camera matching [S25, S29].
- Website implication: state clearly and factually that 18+ products are only released after an age check at
  the machine. This is youth-protection information, not advertising.

### 4.3 Tobacco/e-cigarette advertising (TabakerzG) — **highest website risk**
- § 19 TabakerzG prohibits advertising for tobacco products, e-cigarettes and refill containers in the press
  and in information-society services (websites, social media, newsletters) [S20, S21].
- OLG Bamberg, 21.01.2026, 3 UKl 30/25 e: eight product statements (suitability for beginners and experienced
  users, flavour variety, quality, ease of use) were impermissible advertising [S21].
- OLG Karlsruhe, 08.04.2026, 6 U 1/26: a homepage banner with colourful "splash" backgrounds and "3 NEUE
  FLAVOURS" went beyond neutral product information; the court said nothing makes a retailer's own homepage
  banner acceptable just because it sits inside the shop [S22].
- Only purely factual information (name, pack size, price, flavour designation as data) is tolerated; pull
  vs. push distinctions do not save attractive presentation [S20, S22].
- **Website rule (R4):** at most one factual line in the Sortiment list; no images, no emphasis, no keywords.

### 4.4 Online legal basics
- **Impressum:** § 5 Digitale-Dienste-Gesetz (DDG) since 14 May 2024 (replaced § 5 TMG) [S30].
- **EU ODR link:** the EU online dispute-resolution platform was shut down on 20 July 2025; the link
  obligation fell away and stale links should be removed [S31].
- **Cookies/device access:** § 25 TDDDG (renamed from TTDSG, 14 May 2024); consent required unless strictly
  necessary [S32]. A site with no cookies and no third-party embeds needs no banner.
- **Fonts:** dynamic Google Fonts embedding without consent violated GDPR; €100 damages (LG München I,
  20.01.2022, 3 O 17493/20) [S33]. → self-host.
- **Accessibility (BFSG, applies since 28 June 2025):** micro-enterprises providing services (< 10 staff and
  < €2 m turnover/balance sheet) are exempt [S34]; an information-only site with no online sales is very
  likely outside scope anyway. We build to WCAG 2.2 AA regardless.
- **Video surveillance:** if the shop has CCTV, GDPR Art. 13 information is required at the location; the website
  can host the full notice (template in `06-legal-pages.md`).

### 4.5 Google Business Profile eligibility
Google requires in-person contact during stated hours but explicitly permits ATMs, video-rental kiosks and
express drop boxes if they display contact information for help [S35]. Vending shops are not named.
Practitioner guidance: permanent location, visible support phone, accurate 24-hour hours if always accessible,
one listing per location [S36]. → Show a support phone on site and at the shop; use the site's Kontakt page
as the profile's help reference.

## 5. Strategic insights (what we'd do differently from everyone else)

1. **Sell the place, not the machines.** Competitors photograph machines. AlpenBox24 should photograph the
   *moment*: lit shop on a dark road, snow, mountains at dawn, a hiker's hand taking a Brezn at 05:40.
2. **Make "regional" concrete.** Named producers, village, distance in km, what they supply. Only with consent.
3. **Turn the no-staff worry into a trust page.** "So geht's" answers: how to pay, how age checks work, what
   happens if a machine eats your money, who to call, how fast you get refunded, CCTV and safety.
4. **Own Sunday/holiday intent in content** ("Sonntags einkaufen in Miesbach") — honest, useful, low competition.
5. **Be the legally cleanest shop in the niche.** It protects the client against Abmahnungen and gives a
   municipality nothing to object to. The site's tone should signal responsibility (age checks, cleanliness,
   neighbourly behaviour at night).
6. **Hours as data.** One edit in `site.ts` updates footer, schema, Kontakt, FAQ if Miesbach ever restricts
   Sunday hours.
